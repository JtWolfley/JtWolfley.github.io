Star Wars Legos

About the Legos

Star Wars LEGO first came out in 1999 when LEGO teamed up with Lucasfilm to make sets based on the Star Wars movies. The sets became really popular because they let fans build ships, characters, and scenes from the movies. Over the years, LEGO has made hundreds of Star Wars sets, including some based on newer shows and movies. Today, Star Wars LEGO is one of LEGO’s most popular themes and is enjoyed by both kids and collectors.

Star Wars LEGO sets come in many different sizes and prices. Some sets are small and simple, while others can have thousands of pieces and take many hours to build. Fans can build famous ships such as the Millennium Falcon, X-wing, and Republic Gunship. LEGO also makes minifigures of many different Star Wars characters that can be included with the sets.
